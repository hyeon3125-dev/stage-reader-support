StageReader Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

StageReader imports TXT and DRM-free EPUB files selected by you. Book files, extracted text, reading position, reading preferences, and any SCALAR copy ID found in a selected book stay in the app storage on your device. StageReader does not upload book titles, book text, file hashes, or copy IDs. Displaying a copy ID makes no network request.

Diagnostics and payments

StageReader does not include advertising, analytics, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or an external payment mechanism.

Your choices

You can delete an imported book in Reading Settings or remove all app data by uninstalling StageReader.
