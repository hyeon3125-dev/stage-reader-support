StageReader Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

StageReader imports TXT and DRM-free EPUB files selected by you. Book files, extracted text, reading position, and reading preferences stay in the app storage on your device. StageReader does not upload book titles, book text, or file hashes.

Advertising and diagnostics

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google may collect an IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions, product interactions, crash logs, and performance data for third-party advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service reliability. StageReader requests non-personalized ads by default and does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. Consent and privacy choices are shown where required by local law and can be reopened from Reading Settings.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes the optional one-time ad-removal purchase. StageReader stores only the resulting entitlement on your device. Purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android.

Your choices

You can delete an imported book in Reading Settings, remove all app data by uninstalling StageReader, change advertising consent where required, or purchase ad removal.
