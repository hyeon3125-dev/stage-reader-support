StageReader Support

StageReader reads UTF-8, UTF-16, or EUC-KR TXT files and DRM-free EPUB files. It does not open DRM-protected books, PDF, MOBI, or Kindle store files.

샘플 본문을 복사한 뒤, StageReader에서 ‘텍스트 붙여넣기’를 눌러 본문을 붙여넣고 ‘읽기 시작’을 선택하세요. ‘읽기 설정’에서 ‘읽는 방식’을 ‘집중’으로 바꾸면 한 문단씩 읽을 수 있어요.

Copy the sample text, then choose “Paste text” in StageReader. Paste the text and choose “Start reading”. In “Reading settings”, set “Reading mode” to “Focus” to read one paragraph at a time.

A book will not open

Confirm the extension is .txt or .epub, then try exporting a fresh DRM-free copy. Files over the in-app safety limits are rejected to protect phone memory.

Reading or import issue

Include the file type, approximate file size, device model, and a screenshot if possible. Do not send a copyrighted book file unless you have permission to share it.
